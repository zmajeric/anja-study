# Ocular Disease Glossary

The canonical language for this subject. Every lesson, quiz item and learning record uses these
words in these senses. Where the resources use two words for one thing, this file picks one.

- **Terminology rulings are workspace decisions** and are recorded as soon as the ambiguity is met.
- **Definitions are promoted on evidence**: a term enters once the learner uses it correctly.

Not to be confused with `reference/glossary.html` (once it exists), the printable learner-facing sheet.
