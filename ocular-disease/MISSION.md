# Mission: Ocular Disease (Hochschule Aalen)

## Why
Pass the ocular disease exam on **Friday 13 November 2026**: Prof. Lorne Yudcovitch's twelve lectures
"Ocular Disease Anterior Segment I: Eyelid" (Aalen 2026). The exam is in English. The numbers
(question mix 70% MCQ · 10% written · 20% image, 4 options, 40-question mock exam) live in `subject.json`.

## Success looks like
- Answer every topic on the exam guidelines (`source/Exam review notes.pdf`) and every point the
  professor stressed in his review (`source/AUDIO-2026-09-24-09-29-48.md`).
- Recognise an eyelid condition from a clinical photo and name its diagnosis and treatment
  ("How would you treat it, diagnoses").
- Separate look-alikes fast: anterior vs posterior blepharitis, staph vs Demodex, hordeolum vs
  chalazion, preseptal vs orbital cellulitis, dacryoadenitis vs dacryocystitis, entropion vs
  ectropion, benign vs malignant lid lesions.
- Know the drugs by name: what treats what, and which topical drops cause allergic contact dermatitis.
- Cover all twelve lectures before 13 November with at least one spaced review of each.

## Constraints
- **Sources**: the twelve lectures (HANDOUT and NOTES carry the same slides), the professor's audio
  exam review, and the learner's exam review notes. No textbook.
- **Scope**: teach everything in the lectures (learner's decision, 26 Oct 2026). The guidelines and the
  audio mark what is an exam emphasis; they do not cut anything.
- **The learner's notes** (`*_edited.pdf`, `notes/notes.json`) show what the lecture stressed; a fact
  found only there is labelled "from your notes".
- Image questions use every usable clinical photo from the slides.
- Question banks are wide: many questions per fact, the same fact asked in several ways, so repeated
  quizzes do not repeat.
- Study language: English (the exam language).

## Out of scope
- The other half of the exam paper (25 questions from Dr. Goodwin) belongs to the subject `ocular`.
