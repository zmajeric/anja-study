Demodex folliculorum • Cigar shaped (0.28 - 0.44 mm) • Colonial (3+ per follicle) • Upper section of hair follicle • Collarettes/cuffs – Best clinical diagnostic sign Demodex brevis • Shorter, stubbier species (0.16 - 0.21 mm) • Solitary • Sebaceous glands • Damage/plugging of gland – Dry eye – Chalazion formation

Demodex Lifecycle and Behavior

• Life cycle between 14 to 18 days • Egg  larva  adult • Female mites lay up to 10-12 eggs at a time • Leads to overpopulation if unmanaged • Demodex avoids light; buries head-first in follicles or glands, with only tail visible at lash follicle opening

• Lays eggs, ingests gland lining • Results in follicular base distension and trichiasis • Debris generated collects at lash roots • Appears as gelatinous cylindrical ‘sleeve’ or cylindrical dandruff (CD) at eyelash base (pathognomonic for mite infestation)

Demodex treatment considerations

• Mites lifespan: 2-3 weeks • Female will lay about 20 eggs in single lash follicle in that time • Patients need several weeks (6-8 weeks in some cases) of treatment and maintenance • Immunocompromised patients may retain more mites and for longer time periods

– Mites may produce immunologic suppressants
