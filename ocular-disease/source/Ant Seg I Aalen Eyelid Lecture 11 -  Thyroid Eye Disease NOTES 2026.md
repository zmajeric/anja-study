## Page 62

Clinical Activity Score (CAS)
Proposed Classification System to Assess Disease Activity in TED
• One point is given for each sign present. The sum of these points
defines activity score (From Mourits et al.)
• Pain
• Painful, oppressive feeling on or behind the globe
• Pain on attempted up, side, or down gaze
• Eyelid redness
• Eyelid edema
• Diffuse conjunctival redness
• Chemosis (conjunctiva edema)
• Increase proptosis of 2 mm or more between 1 and 3
months
• Impaired function
• Decrease in visual acuity of 1 or more lines on Snellen chart
(using pinhole) between 1 and 3 months
• Decrease of eye movements in any direction equal to or
more than 5 degrees between 1 and 3 months
