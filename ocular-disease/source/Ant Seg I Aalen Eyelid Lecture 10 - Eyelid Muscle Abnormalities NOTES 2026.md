Congenital Ptosis

•Present at birth •90% ptosis cases •70% unilateral •30% have strabismus •12% have anisometropia >1.00D •20% have amblyopia •Typically deprivational

## Page 51

Blepharospasm

•Subjective •At one end of spectrum - essential blepharospasm - manifested by simple increased blink rate and intermittent eyelid spasms

•On the other end of the spectrum - blepharospasm is disabling condition with ocular pain and functional blindness

•Blepharospasm is commonly associated with dystonic movements of other facial muscles
