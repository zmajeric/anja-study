Staphylococcal Blepharitis

Baby Shampoo – the ‘old’ treatment modality

The baby shampoo is first diluted one-to-one with

water in 'cup' in palm of hand.

Then mixed by rubbing with clean fingertips and

applied in gentle oval scrubbing motion to margin and eyelash base of closed eyelid for 1 minute, followed by fresh water facial rinse.

Baby shampoo can alternatively be diluted in

container (e.g. plastic cup) and scrubbing performed using washcloth wrapped around finger (after dipping it in the diluted shampoo). A cotton tip applicator may be used alternatively.

NOT recommended anymore due to drying effects
