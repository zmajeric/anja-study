Blepharospasm

•Definition: Any abnormal tic or twitch of eyelid

•Bilateral involuntary orbicularis contraction: results in eye closure

•Spasmodic

•Idiopathic

49

Blepharospasm

•Patients over 50

•F>M

•There are at least 50,000 cases of blepharospasm in United States

•Up to 2000 new cases diagnosed annually •Prevalence approximately 5 in 100,000

50

Blepharospasm

•Subjective •At one end of spectrum - essential blepharospasm - manifested by simple increased blink rate and intermittent eyelid spasms

•On the other end of the spectrum - blepharospasm is disabling condition with ocular pain and functional blindness

•Blepharospasm is commonly associated with dystonic movements of other facial muscles
