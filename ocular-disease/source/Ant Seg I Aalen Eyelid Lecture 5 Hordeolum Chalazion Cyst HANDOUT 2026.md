Internal Hordeolum

Drainage of a hordeolum

Stab incision at the site of pointing using an 18-

gauge needle or a #11 blade

External incisions lead to scarring, so making

external eyelid incisions or punctures is inadvisable, unless the hordeolum already is pointing externally

ThA large abscess may have multiple pockets and

require multiple stabs.

Internal incisions should be made vertically to

minimize the area of cornea swept by a scar during blinking

Leave the incision open with a clean margin
