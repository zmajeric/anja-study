A. Disruption of the afferent pathway will affect the direct response of the ipsilateral eye and

the consensual response of the contralateral eye. B. Swinging flashlight test

1. The patient fixates on a distance target, and the doctor moves a light from one eye to the other several times.

2. It is important to make sure each pupil is illuminated with an equal amount of light.

3. If there is no damage to the visual pathway, there will be little or no change in pupil size when switching the light from one eye to the other.

4. If there is damage to the afferent pathway, both pupils will dilate quicker when shining light into the pupil on the side ipsilateral to the damage.
