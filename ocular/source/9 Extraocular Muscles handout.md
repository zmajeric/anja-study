IV. Muscle coordination
A. Synergist/agonist muscles work together to accomplish the same action.
B. Antagonist muscles work in opposite directions.
1. Sherrington’s law of reciprocal innervation: contraction of one muscle is
accompanied by a simultaneous and proportional relaxation of the antagonist muscle.
C. Yoke muscles: the muscles of the two eyes that work together to cause symmetric,
binocular eye movements.
1. Hering’s law of equal innervation: innervation to the muscles of the two eyes is equal
and simultaneous.
