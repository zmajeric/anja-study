## Page 12

B. Fovea (fovea centralis)
1. The bipolar and ganglion cells are displaced laterally
2. 1 mm inferior to the center of the disc
3. Rod free zone
4. Capillary-free zone
C. Ora serrata
1. Peripheral termination of the retina
2. 5 mm anterior to the equator
3. Firm attachment between the retina and vitreous

## Page 13

D. Optic disc aka optic nerve head
1. Ganglion cell axons accumulate and exit the eye
2. The central retinal artery enters and the central retinal vein exits the eye
3. Dimensions
a. 1.7 mm horizontally
b. 1.9 mm vertically
4. Physiologic cup
