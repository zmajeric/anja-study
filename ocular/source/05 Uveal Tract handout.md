III. Ciliary body
A. Characteristics
1. Triangular shape sagittally
2. Ring-shaped structure from iris root to ora serrata
3. Two parts
a. Pars plicata
b. Pars plana
(1) The zonular fibers

1. Smooth muscle
2. Parasympathetic stimulation
3. Sympathetic stimulation has no clinically significant effect on the ciliary body.
C. Ciliary body blood vessels
1. Capillaries are fenestrated with wide lumens
D. Epithelium
1. Inner layer of cells are joined by zonula occludens.

IV. Choroid
A. Choroidal arteries are branches of the short posterior ciliary arteries
1. The blood vessels get smaller as they branch and get closer to the retina.
2. Choriocapillaris
a. Single layer of anastomosing, fenestrated capillaries
b. Densest in the macular area: it is the sole blood supply to the macular area.
3. Choroidal veins join in vortex pattern (vortex veins) in each quadrant of the eye
