# Ocular Anatomy Glossary

The canonical language for this subject. Every lesson, quiz item and learning record uses these
words in these senses. Where the resources use two words for one thing, this file picks one.

Two rules govern what lands here:

- **Terminology rulings are workspace decisions** and are recorded as soon as the ambiguity is
  met, because lessons need a consistent vocabulary from the first one.
- **Definitions are promoted on evidence.** A term enters the list below once the learner can
  use it correctly, not when it is first taught.

Not to be confused with `reference/glossary.html` (once it exists), the printable learner-facing
sheet covering every term in a chapter.

## Rulings

**Crystalline lens**:
The eye's own lens (chapter 4). Lecture 4's handout is titled "Intraocular Lens Anatomy", but the
textbook uses *intraocular lens (IOL)* for the artificial lens implanted after cataract removal
(Textbook · Ch. 7).
_Avoid_: intraocular lens, IOL (for the natural lens)
