# Mission: Ocular Anatomy (Hochschule Aalen)

## Why
Pass the ocular anatomy exam on **Friday 13 November 2026**: Prof. Denise Goodwin's twelve lectures
("Anatomy of the Visual System", Aalen 2026). The exam is in English. The numbers (question mix
80% MCQ · 0% written · 20% image, 4 options, 40-question mock exam) live in `subject.json`;
the learner dropped written questions on 23 Oct 2026.

## Success looks like
- Meet every objective in Prof. Goodwin's exam guidelines (`source/14 Review slide handout.pdf`),
  topic by topic: identify, describe, list, compare as each objective asks.
- Answer MCQs that separate look-alikes quickly (corneal layers, retinal layers, ganglion cell types,
  orbital foramina and fissures, muscle actions, nerve branches).
- Identify a labelled structure on a textbook figure, slide, OCT or gonioscopy image.
- Trace a pathway end to end and predict what a lesion does: aqueous outflow (the learner's notes on the guidelines say it
  will be a flow chart), pupillary light and near responses, sympathetic and parasympathetic routes,
  the visual pathway and its field defects.
- Cover all twelve chapters before 13 November, with at least one spaced review of each.

## Constraints
- **Sources**: the textbook (Remington & Goodwin, 4th ed.), the twelve lecture handouts and the twelve
  slide decks, and the exam guidelines. Handouts and slides are both learning material.
- **Scope**: what is taught is set by the guidelines' objectives and the points in the handouts and on the
  slides. The textbook explains those points; textbook detail that serves none of them is not taught
  (e.g. its research on contact lens wear).
- **The learner's notes** (`*_edited.pdf`, the guidelines' typed notes) show what the lecture stressed. Facts
  come from the textbook, handouts and slides; a fact found only in the notes is labelled "from your notes".
- Image questions use figures from the textbook and the slides.
- Study language: English (the exam language).

## Out of scope
- Textbook chapter 9, Ocular Embryology, beyond what the lens lecture itself covers (no lecture).
- The guidelines' **Circulation** topic and textbook chapter 12, Orbital Blood Supply, beyond what the
  handouts and slides already cover (e.g. retinal and uveal blood supply in chapters 5 and 7): no lecture,
  so no chapter of its own (learner's decision, 20 Oct 2026).
