# Histology Resources

## Knowledge

- [Study script: _HISTOLOGY — A Study Script for the Examination_ (Histology_Study_Script.docx)](./Histology_Study_Script.docx)
  Bilingual EN/SLO script written from Prof. Thomas F. Freddo's lectures at Hochschule Aalen. Nine chapters: 1 Blood cells & hematopoiesis · 2 Blood & lymphatic vessels · 3 The cell · 4 Embryonic tissues · 5 Epithelia · 6 Neural cells & glia · 7 Skeletal & smooth muscle · 8 Cartilage & bone · 9 Soft connective tissues. Each chapter ends with 6–8 practice questions written by the script author (not real past papers). **This is the sole teaching source by the learner's instruction.** Plain-text extraction: [`source/Histology_Study_Script.md`](./source/Histology_Study_Script.md) (re-extract from the .docx if the .docx changes).
- [Prof. Freddo's lecture slide decks, chapters 1–9](./source/) — `1 Blood Cells Aalen.pdf` … `9 Soft CT. Aalen.pdf`.
  The primary material the script was written from. Used as the **source of every micrograph** in the picture questions (`assets/img/*.jpg`, extracted and cropped from the slides; image → slide page mapping in `NOTES.md`). Text is not taught from the slides where the script already covers it.
- [Sample exam questions 2026](./source/sample-2026.docx) — three histology items showing the real format: a micrograph + "Identify the cell shown" / "What type of muscle cell is shown?" with 3–4 one-to-three-word options. The picture questions in `assets/bank/*.js` copy this format.
- Script, Chapter 4 "Further Study Resources": Prof. Freddo recommends three Khan Academy videos (oogenesis, spermatogenesis, zygote-to-neurulation). Mentioned in the script itself, so allowed, but not used unless the learner asks.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- None sought. The learner's instruction is to stay within the script; classmates on the Aalen course are the natural community if wanted.

## Gaps

- No real past exam papers exist publicly (the script author says so). MCQ style is inferred from the script's practice questions and the three sample-2026 items.
- Chapters 3 (cell) and 4 (embryology) have almost no micrographs in the slides — only three organelle EMs and one cilium EM. Their picture banks are correspondingly small or empty.
- The script has a few internally inconsistent numbers (see `NOTES.md`); both values are taught since either may appear in a question.
