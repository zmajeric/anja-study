Soft connective tissue

Cells, fibres, gel, categories, eye examples.

Cells

Fibrocyte = ACTIVE (ovoid nucleus, many processes; makes all ECM; induces chemokines) · Fibroblast = INACTIVE (fusiform, few processes, flat dark nucleus) — Freddo's convention. Mast cell: boundaries (skin, mucosa, conjunctiva); histamine granules; IgE receptors; degranulation = granule–membrane fusion; Pataday (olopatadine 0.2%) stabilises the membrane. Macrophage: from monocytes; histiocyte, Kupffer, alveolar, microglia = mononuclear phagocyte/RES; antigen presentation to T cells. Plasma cell = B derivative. Adipocyte: empty hole after processing (fat dissolved), thin rim, flat nucleus; white (hypodermis; storage, cushioning) vs brown (around organs; insulation); Oil Red O on frozen section.

Ground substance

Hyaluronan backbone → link protein → core protein → GAGs (chondroitin, dermatan, heparan, keratan sulfate); core + GAGs = proteoglycan. Large: versican, perlecan, neurocan, aggrecan (cartilage, sclera), brevican. Small: decorin, biglycan, testican, bikunin, fibromodulin, lumican. Hyaluronan: non-sulfated; made in plasma membrane; huge; water sponge; 15 g/70 kg, ⅓ turned over daily; vitreous = hyaluronan gel.
