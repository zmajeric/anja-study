Histology glossary

Terms exactly as the script uses them. Slovenian in brackets where the script gives it.

Chapter 1 — Blood cells and hematopoiesis

Formed elements (formirani elementi krvi)
The cellular fraction of blood: erythrocytes, platelets, leukocytes.
Eosinophilic / acidophilic
Takes up the acidic dye eosin → pink. Opposite: basophilic — takes up the basic dye (methylene blue) → blue/purple. Neutrophilic = neither.
Anucleate
Having no nucleus (erythrocytes, platelets).
Biconcave disc
Erythrocyte shape; 7.2 µm; the thin centre looks pale and increases surface area for gas exchange.
Reticulocyte (retikulocit)
Immature RBC released early from marrow; blue RNA strands/dots; ~1 per 100 RBC; matures in 1–2 days.
Platelet / thrombocyte (trombocit)
Anucleate fragment pinched off a megakaryocyte; 2–3 µm; 5–11 days; hemostasis.
Megakaryocyte
Large marrow cell whose surface sheds platelets.
Marginal microtubule band
Ring of microtubules around the platelet rim; keeps the resting disc shape and contracts on activation.
Open canalicular system
Membrane channels in the platelet continuous with its surface.
α / δ / λ granules
Platelet storage granules: α = proteoglycans + hemostasis factors; δ (dense bodies) = serotonin + Ca++; λ = lysosomes.
Wright stain
Eosin + methylene blue; routine smears.
Giemsa stain
Eosin + methylene blue + Azure B; preferred if systemic microbial disease suspected; Azure B highlights reticulocytes.
Granulocyte / agranulocyte
Leukocytes with / without visible specific cytoplasmic granules. Granulocytes: neutrophil, eosinophil, basophil. Agranulocytes: monocyte, lymphocyte.
Differential WBC count
Percentage of each leukocyte type; basis of clinical blood interpretation.
Band neutrophil (paličasti nevtrofilec)
Immature neutrophil with horseshoe-shaped, unsegmented nucleus; normally 3–5%.
Left shift
Increased band neutrophils → marrow releasing more WBCs; usually infection or inflammation.
Diapedesis
Leukocyte (e.g. monocyte) leaving the bloodstream through the vessel wall.
Plasma cell
Antibody-producing cell derived from a B lymphocyte; not found circulating.
Histiocyte
Tissue macrophage derived from an extravascular monocyte.
Hematopoiesis (hematopoeza)
Production of all formed elements from one multipotential stem cell.
Hemocytoblast
The multipotential, self-renewing hematopoietic stem cell.
Common myeloid / lymphoid progenitor
The two great lines of descent. Myeloid → megakaryocyte, erythrocyte, mast cell, myeloblast. Lymphoid → NK cell, small lymphocyte (T, B).
Myeloblast
Myeloid cell giving rise to basophil, neutrophil, eosinophil and monocyte.
Bursa of Fabricius
Bird organ where B cells mature; in humans the equivalent is the bone marrow. T cells mature in the Thymus.
Multipotential
Able to give rise to every formed element; said of the hemocytoblast, which also self-renews.
Natural killer (NK) cell
A large granular lymphocyte from the common lymphoid progenitor; branches off separately from the small lymphocyte.
Yolk sac
Site of embryonic hematopoiesis (islands of cells); after birth, the bone marrow takes over.
Interleukins (IL-1 … IL-12)
Signalling molecules that, with colony stimulating factors, TNF-α and TGF-β, regulate each branch point of hematopoiesis. IL-5 is the eosinophil signature.
SCF, FLT-3 ligand, SDF-1
Stem cell factor (most myeloid steps); FLT-3 ligand and SDF-1 (stem cell → lymphoid progenitor).
Colony stimulating factors (G-CSF, M-CSF, GM-CSF)
Growth factors driving granulocyte / monocyte / both lineages.
TPO, Epo
Thrombopoietin (→ megakaryocyte/platelets); erythropoietin (→ erythrocytes).
Hypoxia
Low oxygen delivery; the stimulus for EPO production and release.
Erythropoietin (EPO) (eritropoetin)
Glycoprotein hormone/cytokine from kidney interstitial fibroblasts and liver peri-sinusoidal cells; released by hypoxia; drives red cell production. Recombinant EPO treats anemia (IBD blood loss, chemotherapy, renal dialysis).

Chapter 2 — Vessels

Tunica intima / media / adventitia
The three concentric layers of every vessel wall, lumen outward: endothelium + subendothelium; smooth muscle / elastic membranes; connective tissue with vasa vasorum and nerves.
Internal / external elastic lamina
Elastin sheets marking the outer border of the intima and of the media. Both dark and distinct in a muscular artery; the internal one is wavy from post-mortem smooth-muscle contraction.
Vasa vasorum
"Vessels of the vessels": small vessels within the wall of thick vessels such as the aorta. Their torn lumen openings start a dissecting aneurysm.
Elastic / muscular artery, arteriole
Aorta and proximal great vessels (40–70 fenestrated elastic membranes; feel systole) · named pulse-bearing vessels (up to 40 muscle layers, thick IEL) · ≤150 µm, 1–2 muscle layers, no pulse.
Capillary types
Continuous (tight junctions: iris, retina) · fenestrated (choroid, ciliary body) · discontinuous (liver sinusoid, glomerulus).
Pericyte
Contractile mural cell within splits of the endothelial basement membrane (smooth muscle lies outside it); round nucleus bulging from retinal capillaries.
Post-capillary venule
First venous segment; pericytes; site of leukocyte emigration and inflammatory leakage.
Venous valve
Paired fold of intima; present below the heart only; works with the muscle pump (soleus = "second heart").
AV nicking
Focal venous constriction at a retinal arteriovenous crossing: shared adventitia + arteriolar sclerosis.
Endocardium / myocardium / pericardium
Heart equivalents of intima / media (striated cardiac muscle) / adventitia. Pericardium = fibrous + serous (parietal; visceral = epicardium).

Chapter 3 — The cell

Prokaryote / eukaryote
No nucleus, nucleoid, binary fission, cell wall (antibiotic target) / true nucleus, membrane-bound organelles, mitosis and meiosis.
Virus
Not a cell: DNA or RNA in a capsid (± envelope); replicates only in a host; RNA viruses carry reverse transcriptase; bacteriophage infects bacteria.
Nucleolus
Nuclear region holding the rRNA genes.
Microfilaments / microtubules / intermediate filaments
F-actin / tubulin (transport, division, cilia) / desmin, GFAP, vimentin, syncoilin (neurofilaments in neurons).
RER / SER
Ribosome-studded cisternae for protein synthesis and folding / no ribosomes, lipid synthesis (sarcoplasmic reticulum in muscle).
Transcription / translation
DNA → mRNA, same language / mRNA → amino acids, new language, at the RER (30S decodes, 50S catalyses).
Golgi cis / trans
Receives from the ER / releases secretory vesicles. Always next to the nucleus.
Cristae
Folds of the inner mitochondrial membrane. Mitochondrial DNA is maternal; brown fat mitochondria make heat.
Centrosome
Pair of centrioles (microtubule arrays at 90°); organises the mitotic spindle.
Primary / secondary lysosome, phagosome
Acidic Golgi-derived vesicle / its fusion with a phagosome, where digestion happens / vesicle from endocytosis, autophagocytosis or phagocytosis.
Hurler's syndrome
Lysosomal storage disease: mucopolysaccharides accumulate, including in the cornea.

Chapter 4 — Embryonic tissues

Zona pellucida / corona radiata
Glycoprotein coat of the egg / follicle cells outside it.
Acrosome / cortical reaction
Sperm enzymes digest the zona / egg granules destroy the sperm-binding site — the block to polyspermy.
9 + 2 / 9 + 0
Motile cilium (sperm tail) / non-motile sensory cilium (photoreceptor connecting cilium).
Cleavage, morula, compaction
Division without growth in the Fallopian tube; 32-cell mulberry ends cleavage; cells press together.
Blastocyst
Trophoblasts (→ placenta) around a blastocoel and an eccentric inner cell mass / embryoblasts (→ embryo).
Epiblast / hypoblast; bilaminar disc; primitive streak
Upper / lower layer of the two-layered embryo; the streak sets the body axes.
Gastrulation; endoderm, mesoderm, ectoderm
Migration through the streak makes the three germ layers (trilaminar disc), all from epiblast.
Notochord
Mesodermal rod that induces the neural plate; persists only as the nucleus pulposus.
Neuroectoderm; neural plate, groove, tube, crest
Region of ectoderm committed to nervous tissue; the tube closes from the midpoint outward; crest cells migrate widely (PNS, anterior eye).
Anencephaly / spina bifida (occulta, meningocele, myelomeningocele)
Closure failure above / below the midpoint.
Ectasia / staphyloma
Eye-wall bulge without / with uveal tissue (keratoconus / blue-grey sclera).

Chapter 5 — Epithelia

Simple / stratified / pseudostratified / transitional
One layer / many layers from a basal layer (named for the surface cells; mitosis only basal) / one layer of unequal heights / changes with organ filling.
Squamous / cuboidal / columnar
Flat (fusiform nuclei; hexagonal en face) / box (central nuclei) / tall (basal nuclei).
Keratinized / non-keratinized
Surface cells lose nuclei, dry (skin) / keep nuclei, moist (cornea, mouth, oesophagus, anal canal, vagina).
Myoepithelium
Epithelial cell with a basal smooth-muscle-like part; gland ducts; iris dilator (AME on PPE).
Axoneme, basal body, transition zone
Microtubule core of a cilium; its triplet anchor; the junction between.
Ciliopathy (Bardet–Biedl)
Defective cilia: polydactyly + RP-like retinal degeneration.
Melanocyte, Langerhans cell, goblet cell, M cell
Pigment via melanosomes / antigen presentation (none in central cornea) / unicellular mucus gland (fornix) / mucosal antigen presentation.
Zonular / focal junction
Belt around the cell (can seal) / spot (can only fasten).
Zonula occludens (tight junction)
Claudins, occludins, ZO-1 → actin; barrier (blood-ocular barriers) + fence (polarity).
Zonula adherens
Ca++-dependent cadherins + catenins → F-actin; sheet tension.
Gap junction, connexon, connexin
Six connexins = connexon; two connexons = gated channel; electrical and metabolic coupling.
Desmosome (macula adherens)
Desmoglein/desmocollin, desmoplakin plaques → intermediate filaments (tonofibrils); also in muscle.
Hemidesmosome
Keratins 5/14 → plectin, BP230 → collagen XVII, integrin α6β4 → laminin-332 → collagen VII; attaches basal cells to the basal lamina.
Lamina lucida / lamina densa; anchoring fibrils
Electron-lucent layer with laminin-332 / dense core / collagen VII loops into Bowman's layer.
Epidermolysis bullosa; pemphigoid; EBMD; recurrent erosion
Inherited split at the missing molecule / acquired autoimmune BM attack with scarring / integrin defect, map-dot-fingerprint / unrepaired hemidesmosomes, morning abrasion.

Chapter 6 — Neurons and glia

Grey / white matter
Cell bodies / myelinated axons. Brain: grey outside; cord: grey inside.
Soma (perikaryon), axon, dendrite, dendritic spine
Cell body / single output / many inputs / synaptic contact site.
Nissl bodies
Basophilic clumps of rough ER in the soma.
Myelin; node of Ranvier; oligodendrocyte / Schwann cell
Insulating spiral wrap; unmyelinated gap where depolarisation occurs; CNS (several axons) / PNS (one axon) myelinator.
Lamina cribrosa
Where ganglion-cell axons become myelinated; nerve diameter doubles (~1.5 → 3 mm).
Myelinated retinal nerve fibre layer
Congenital myelin in the retina → absolute scotoma of the same shape.
Anterograde / retrograde axoplasmic flow
Soma → synapse (slow mm/day; fast ~100×) / back to soma.
Motor end plate; motor unit
Neuromuscular junction / a neuron plus all its muscle cells.
Astrocyte (protoplasmic 1A/1B, fibrous, radial)
Support and regulation; vessel feet; gap-junction syncytium; 1B (GFAP + NCAM) dominates the optic nerve head.
Microglia
Resident CNS/retinal macrophages; activated early in glaucoma.
Müller cell
Radial retinal glia from ILM to ELM; K+, glycogen, transmitter uptake, support, light guide.

Chapter 7 — Muscle

Skeletal / cardiac / smooth
Long multinucleate striated / branching uninucleate striated with intercalated discs / fusiform uninucleate unstriated.
Endomysium / perimysium / epimysium
Around fibre / fascicle / whole muscle.
Sarcomere; Z-disc, A band, I band, H zone, M line
Z to Z; thick-filament length (constant); no-myosin zone (narrows); non-overlap (narrows); centre.
Actin, tropomyosin, troponin (TnC, TnT, TnI)
Thin filament: binding site / blocker / Ca++ sensor that moves the blocker.
Myosin heavy chain, hinge, light chains
Thick filament: head-bearing chain / pivot / MLC-1 essential, MLC-2 regulatory.
Titin / α-actinin
Anchor thick / thin filaments to the Z-disc.
Cross-bridge cycle
Attach → working stroke → ATP binds, detach → ATP split, re-cock; one ATP each.
T-tubule, triad, dyad, sarcoplasmic reticulum
Membrane invagination; T-tubule + two cisternae (skeletal, A–I junction); one cisterna (cardiac, Z line); Ca++ store.
Ryanodine receptor, SERCA, sequestrin
Ca++ release channel / re-uptake pump / loose store protein.
Pre-load / after-load
Length–tension (optimal overlap) / force–velocity (load slows cycling).
Tetany / tetanus
Hypocalcemic depolarisation / C. tetani tetanospasmin blocking inhibitory impulses.
Unitary / multiunit smooth muscle
Gap-junction coupled (iris sphincter) / individual units, dense autonomic innervation (ciliary muscle).
Calmodulin, myosin light-chain kinase
Smooth-muscle Ca++ switch: Ca–CaM activates MLCK → myosin phosphorylation.
Myofibroblast
Vimentin, α-SMA, palladin; fibrosis and wound contraction; pericytes are myofibroblast-like.
Lacy vacuolization
Glycogen in diabetic myoepithelium (iris dilator) → poor dilation; sphincter unaffected.

Chapter 8 — Cartilage and bone

Chondroblast / chondrocyte; lacuna
Young secreting / mature cartilage cell; the matrix space it sits in.
Aggrecan
Chondroitin-sulfate proteoglycan aggregating with hyaluronan; ~10% of cartilage; also in sclera.
Hyaline / fibrocartilage / elastic cartilage
Glassy, Type II collagen, perichondrium / dense collagen layers, strongest, no perichondrium / elastic network, perichondrium.
Perichondrium / periosteum / endosteum
Cartilage cover (nourishment) / bone outer cover (fibrous + cambium; vascular; nociceptive) / bone inner lining.
Isoindicial
Same refractive index — why fibrocartilage collagen is invisible by light microscopy.
Epiphysis, metaphysis, diaphysis
End, neck (epiphyseal line), shaft of a long bone.
Compact / spongy (cancellous) bone; trabecula
Osteon-built dense shell / meshwork of bony struts with marrow between.
Osteon (Haversian system); Haversian canal; lamella; canaliculus; Volkmann's canal
Unit of compact bone; central canal with arteriole, venule, nerve; concentric ring; osteocyte-process channel; transverse connecting canal.
Osteoprogenitor, osteoblast, lining cell, osteocyte, osteoclast
Reserve / osteoid-maker / flattened old osteoblast / trapped maintainer and mechanosensor / multinucleate resorber from monocytes.
Osteoid; hydroxyapatite; osteonectin; osteocalcin
Uncalcified matrix; bone mineral (~70%); anchors mineral to collagen; binds calcium.
Howship's lacuna; ruffled border
Scalloped bay of an osteoclast; its resorbing surface (carbonic anhydrase → acid).
Endochondral / intramembranous ossification
Bone replacing a cartilage model (long bones) / bone on fibrous tissue (skull flat bones, mandible, maxilla, clavicles).
Parathyroid hormone / calcitonin
Stimulates / inhibits bone resorption.

Chapter 9 — Soft connective tissue

Fibrocyte / fibroblast (Freddo)
ACTIVE (ovoid nucleus, many processes) / INACTIVE (fusiform, few processes). Follow his convention.
Mast cell; degranulation; mast cell stabilizer
Histamine-granule cell with IgE receptors at body boundaries / granule–membrane fusion on allergen / drug (olopatadine) that prevents fusion.
Mononuclear phagocyte (reticuloendothelial) system
All monocyte-derived macrophages: histiocytes, Kupffer cells, alveolar macrophages, microglia.
White / brown fat; Oil Red O
Hypodermal storage and cushioning / peri-organ insulation; lipid stain on frozen sections.
Collagen types I–V
I skin, tendon, bone · II cartilage · III reticulin · IV basal lamina · V cell surfaces, hair, placenta.
Elastic fibre; fibrillin
Stretch 1.5× and recoil; microfibril protein with amorphous elastin.
Reticulin
Type III collagen; silver stain, PAS+; scaffold of liver, marrow, lymphoid organs.
Ground substance; proteoglycan; GAG; link protein; core protein
Gel between fibres; core protein + GAGs; sulfated chains (chondroitin, dermatan, heparan, keratan); joins core proteins to hyaluronan.
Hyaluronan
Non-sulfated GAG made in the plasma membrane; water sponge; the vitreous matrix.
Loose areolar / adipose / reticular; dense regular / irregular / elastic
Six categories. Eye: conjunctival stroma (areolar) · corneal stroma (regular) · sclera (irregular) · Bruch's membrane (elastic).
Chemosis
Oedema of the loose bulbar conjunctival stroma.
