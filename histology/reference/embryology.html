Embryonic tissues

The sequence, the layers, the eye.

Fertilization

Egg: largest cell; zona pellucida (glycoprotein) → corona radiata (follicle cells); ~¼ million mitochondria. Sperm: head (nucleus + acrosome), mid piece (centriole + mitochondrial sheath, ~75–100 mitochondria), tail (flagellum, axial filament, 9 + 2), end piece. Steps: zona binding → acrosome reaction → cortical reaction (block to polyspermy; polyspermy = triploid = not viable) → chromatid delivery. Mitochondrial DNA is maternal.

Cilia: motile = 9 + 2 (dynein arms, nexin bridges, radial links); non-motile = 9 + 0, e.g. the photoreceptor connecting cilium (conducts, doesn't beat). Photoreceptor: outer segment → connecting cilium/basal body → inner segment (mitochondria, ER, Golgi) → nucleus → axon → synapse.

The stages

StageKey facts
Cleavagedivision without growth; blastomeres shrink; in Fallopian tube; morula at 32 cells = end of cleavage; compaction. Day 1 first division, day 2 four cells, day 3–4 morula, day 6 blastocyst, day 7–10 implantation; polar bodies visible.
Blastulationtrophoblasts (outer, nourish → placenta) vs embryoblasts (inner cell mass → embryo); blastocoel; blastocyst; zona dissolves; implantation.
Bilaminar discday 8–9: amniotic cavity; hypoblast (towards blastocoel, continuous with yolk sac) + epiblast; cytotrophoblast + syncytiotrophoblast (invasive; lacunae); Heuser's membrane; coagulation plug. Primitive streak in the epiblast sets the body axes; prochordal plate = future mouth.
Gastrulationthrough the streak: 1st wave → endoderm (displaces hypoblast), 2nd wave → mesoderm, remaining surface → ectoderm. All from epiblast. Trilaminar disc.
Neurulation3rd week. Notochord (mesoderm) = inductive signpost → nucleus pulposus only. Neural plate = neuroectoderm (a region of ectoderm, not a 4th layer). Folds → groove → tube; border cells = neural crest (→ most of PNS). Closure starts at the midpoint, proceeds cranially and caudally; neuropores close last. Somites and coelom from mesoderm.

Derivatives

Endoderm: respiratory tract, lungs, GI tract, liver, pancreas. Mesoderm: muscles incl. heart, skeleton, genital + urinary tracts. Surface ectoderm: skin, adnexa; lens, anterior eye epithelium. Neuroectoderm: CNS; optic cup (inner + outer layers). Neural crest: PNS; anterior segment stroma and angle (1st wave deep to surface ectoderm; 2nd wave anterior + posterior/angle).

The eye: surface ectoderm + neuroectoderm + mesoderm + neural crest — NOT endoderm.

Closure failures and the eye analogy

Above midpoint → anencephaly (lethal). Below → spina bifida: occulta (tuft of hair) · meningocele (dura, no cord) · myelomeningocele (dura + cord, severe). Eye: ectasia = bulge WITHOUT uvea (keratoconus) · staphyloma = bulge WITH uvea (blue-grey sclera).

Exocrine glands and the gut tube

Start at the target epithelial surface → rod grows down → branches (acini/tubules) → canalises (duct). Foregut (coeliac a.): Rathke's pouch, pharyngeal pouches 1–4, lung bud, oesophagus, stomach, liver, gallbladder, pancreatic buds. Midgut (sup. mesenteric a.): vitelline duct, cecal bud. Hindgut (inf. mesenteric a.): cloaca, allantois.
