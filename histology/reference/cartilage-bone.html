Cartilage & bone

Rigid connective tissues on one sheet.

Cartilage

Avascular (diffusion; transplantable like the cornea); chondroblasts → chondrocytes in lacunae; ~10% aggrecan (also sclera), ~75% water, collagen. Roles: airway framework, articular surfaces, endochondral template (growth plates on X-ray). Perichondrium: hyaline + elastic only.

Bone

Matrix + hydroxyapatite. Functions: support, protection, movement, mineral homeostasis, blood cell production. Long bone: epiphysis (articular cartilage), metaphysis (epiphyseal line), diaphysis (medullary cavity); compact shell, spongy (trabeculae + red marrow) ends; nutrient artery/foramen. Periosteum: fibrous (fibroblasts) + cambium (osteoblast progenitors); vascular; nociceptive → fracture pain. Endosteum inside.

Osteon: lamellae around Haversian canal (arteriole, venule, nerve); lacunae with osteocytes; canaliculi with processes + gap junctions; Volkmann's canals transverse; external circumferential lamellae; perforating fibres. Ground section: canals dark circles, lacunae dark ovals, canaliculi hairs.

Matrix: ~30% organic (proteoglycans, GAGs, glycoproteins, osteonectin anchors mineral to collagen, osteocalcin binds Ca); collagen ~90% Type I (+V); ~25% water; ~70% hydroxyapatite. Osteoid = uncalcified.

Ossification: endochondral (cartilage model → long bones, most skeleton) · intramembranous (fibrous template → skull flat bones, mandible, maxilla, clavicles). Remodeling: mechanical stress + hormones; PTH stimulates resorption, calcitonin inhibits.

Cartilage vs bone

Only bone: calcium phosphate, vascular canals, periosteum/endosteum, pain fibres (periosteum), remodeling, hormonal control. Cartilage: 75% water/10% aggrecan; bone: 25% water/30% organic/70% mineral.
