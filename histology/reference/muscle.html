Sheaths: endomysium (fibre) → perimysium (fascicle; nerves, vessels, marbling) → epimysium (muscle) → tendon. Motor unit = nerve branch + its cells. Excitation ≠ contraction.

Sarcomere

Z to Z = A + 2 half I. A band = thick filament length (constant). I band = no myosin, straddles Z (narrows). H zone = non-overlap (narrows). M line = centre. Titin anchors thick, α-actinin thin. Thick = myosin (MHC head, α-helical tail, hinge, MLC-1 essential, MLC-2 regulatory). Thin = actin + tropomyosin (blocks) + troponin. Cross-bridge cycle: attach (ADP+Pi) → working stroke (release) → ATP binds, detach → ATP split, re-cock. 1 ATP each.

Relaxation: SERCA pumps Ca++ into SR, sequestrin holds it; Na+–Ca2+ exchanger exports. AP: Na+ in = depolarise; K+ out = repolarise. Pre-load: optimal overlap = max tension (stretch first). After-load: more load → slower → stops.

Clinical

Myasthenia gravis: eye first in ~50%; fluctuating ptosis + diplopia, worse with fatigue; ocular MG. Caffeine → ryanodine sensitivity → shakes. Hypokalemia (K): weakness, myalgia, tremor. Hypocalcemia (Ca): tetany (Na+ leak, depolarisation). Hyponatremia = Na. Tetanus ≠ tetany: C. tetani → tetanospasmin blocks inhibitory impulses at NMJ → spasm; lock-jaw; 10% die; spatula test; Tdap q10y. Diabetes: myoepithelial dilator gets lacy glycogen vacuoles → poor dilation; sphincter (true smooth) normal.

Eye

EOM: fast (defined myofibrils; thick myelinated nerve, single NMJ) + slow/tonic (poor myofibrils; thin nerve, grape-like NMJs); cholinergic; smallest motor units → precision + yoked relaxation. Global layer (attached to globe) 62% fast; orbital 81% fast. Smooth: unitary (gap junctions: GI, bladder, uterus, ureter, iris sphincter) vs multiunit (no gap junctions, dense autonomic: ciliary muscle, vas deferens); vascular both. Mueller's muscle keeps the lid open. Myoepithelium: keratin+ epithelial cells with basal α-SMA processes, sympathetic; gland ducts, iris dilator. Myofibroblasts: vimentin, α-SMA (ACTA2), palladin; fibrosis, wound contraction; TGF-β1 + ED-A fibronectin + tension; pericytes are myofibroblast-like.
