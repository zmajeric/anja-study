Epithelia

Classification, cilia, interspersed cells, junctions, basement membrane.

Rules

Separates two environments; polarised (apical / basal + basement membrane); always sensory nerves; always avascular. Classify by layers × surface-cell shape. Stratified: named for the surface cells; mitosis only basal; one shared basement membrane on one side (a bilayer of two simple epithelia is not stratified — ciliary epithelium).

Corneal endothelium: stromal water control; specular microscopy; not replaced. RPE: melanin, disc phagocytosis, visual cycle, outer blood-retinal barrier (tight junctions).

Cilia

Axoneme (microtubules) on a basal body (triplets) via a transition zone. 9 + 2 motile (trachea, sperm, Fallopian tube) · 9 + 0 non-motile = photoreceptor connecting cilium; outer segment = specialised cilium. Bardet–Biedl: ciliopathy — polydactyly + RP-like degeneration.

Interspersed cells

Melanocytes (basal; melanosomes into keratinocytes; colour = activity not number) · Langerhans (antigen presentation; skin/conj common, peripheral cornea some, central cornea NONE → immune privilege) · Goblet (unicellular mucus gland; fornix; inner tear layer) · M cells.

Basement membrane and diseases

Lamina lucida (laminin-332) · lamina densa (core) · collagen VII anchoring fibrils into Bowman's layer. "Basal cells attach to the basal lamina by hemidesmosomes; cell-to-cell contact is by desmosomes anchored by tonofibrils."

EB (inherited; split at the missing molecule): KRT5/14, PLEC1, DST → simplex · COL17A1, ITGA6/ITGB4, LAMA3/B3/C2 → junctional · COL7A1 → dystrophic. Ocular cicatricial pemphigoid: acquired autoimmune; scarring, symblepharon. EBMD (map-dot-fingerprint): integrin defect, duplicated BM, erosions. Recurrent corneal erosion: unrepaired hemidesmosomes after paper/fingernail injury; morning onset; fluorescein. Cornea labels: α3β1 lateral · α6β4 basal (EBMD) · laminin 5 (LASEK plane) · collagen VII (ageing, erosion).
