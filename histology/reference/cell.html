The cell

Every organelle: job, cue, exam hook.

Prokaryote vs eukaryote vs virus

Shared: DNA, plasma membrane, cytoplasm, ribosomes. Prokaryote: nucleoid (no envelope), single DNA ring, one gene copy, no organelles, binary fission, rigid cell wall → antibiotic target. Eukaryote: nuclear envelope, two gene copies, membrane-bound organelles, mitosis/meiosis, no wall (vertebrates). Virus: not a cell; DNA or RNA in a capsid (± envelope); replicates only in a host; RNA viruses carry reverse transcriptase; bacteriophages infect bacteria.

Membrane and nucleus

Phospholipid bilayer: polar heads out, nonpolar tails in — "railroad tracks" on EM; proteins (receptors, ports, channels, pumps, adhesion), cholesterol, glycolipids/glycoproteins outside. Nucleus: chromatin · nucleolus (rRNA genes) · nuclear envelope (double) · nuclear pores.
