# Mission: Histology (first year, Hochschule Aalen)

## Why
Pass the first-year histology exam on **Sunday 20 September 2026** (Prof. Thomas F. Freddo's lectures, optometry programme). The exam is in English, roughly 90% multiple choice and 10% short written answers. Six days of 15–20 minute sessions are available.

## Success looks like
- Answer MCQs that discriminate between look-alike options (cell types, epithelium types, junctions, vessel types, collagen types) quickly and correctly.
- Write a 3–5 sentence answer to a "describe / compare / explain why" question in the style of the script's practice questions.
- Recall the key numbers the script says to memorise (percentages, sizes, lifespans, layer counts).
- Recognise the ocular examples and clinical notes for every chapter — these are Freddo's favourite exam points.
- Cover all nine chapters of the script before Sunday, with at least one spaced review of each.

## Constraints
- **Only source: `Histology_Study_Script.docx`** (text copy in `source/`). Do not teach beyond it unless explicitly asked.
- Sessions are 15–20 minutes: one lesson = one chunk + one quiz.
- Six days total (14–19 Sept study, 20 Sept exam). Rough plan in `NOTES.md`.
- Study language: English (the exam language). Slovenian lines in the script are comprehension aids only.

## Out of scope
- Anything not in the script (other textbooks, slide atlases, physiology beyond what the script states).
- Deep microscopy skills beyond the recognition cues the script itself gives.
