1. Two classes, five junctions

Two sentences to memorise exactly

1. Basal cells are attached to the basal lamina by hemidesmosomes.
2. Epithelial cell-to-cell contact is made through desmosomes (macula adherens), anchored intracellularly by tonofibrils.

Clinical notes — four corneal stories

Ocular cicatricial pemphigoid (mucous membrane pemphigoid): acquired, autoimmune attack on the basement membrane zone (EB is inherited). Redness, pain, tearing, photophobia → scarring, tear-film loss, symblepharon, ulcers, perforation, vision loss — hence "cicatricial".

Epithelial basement membrane disease (map-dot-fingerprint dystrophy): improper integrin development → duplicated, misplaced basement membrane → maps, dots, fingerprint lines on retroillumination; poor adherence → erosions.

Recurrent corneal erosion: previous shearing injury (paper, fingernail) → hemidesmosomes not properly repaired → new epithelium never bonds → episodes on first opening the eyes in the morning (lids adhere overnight, first blink pulls it away); fluorescein shows the denuded area.
