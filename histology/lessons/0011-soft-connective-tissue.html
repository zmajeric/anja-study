0. Warm-up — cartilage and bone

1. Why it matters; what it is made of

Soft connective tissue is the body's passageway — beneath every epithelium and within muscle and nerve fascicles, the corridor for nerves and vessels. Loose CT is the immune system's battlefield (antigen recognition and presentation; the reticuloendothelial system), and its matrix filters macromolecules. Every connective tissue = cells + extracellular matrix; ECM = structural proteins + ground substance.

2. The cells

CellFacts
Fibrocyte / fibroblastOne cell line, two states. Freddo's convention: fibrocyte = ACTIVE (ovoid nucleus, multiple processes, plump and busy); fibroblast = INACTIVE (thin, fusiform, few processes, flat dark nucleus). Active cells make all the ECM — collagens, proteoglycans, GAGs, reticular and elastic fibres — and induce chemokine synthesis that calls in immune cells
Mast cellaround vessels and nerves at body boundaries (skin, lung and gut mucosa, mouth, conjunctiva, nose); granules of preformed vasoactive mediators — histamine; IgE receptors; allergen → degranulation (granule–membrane fusion). Stabilise the membrane and nothing is released
Macrophagefrom monocytes; large amoeboid phagocytes; histiocytes, Kupffer cells, alveolar macrophages, microglia = one family, the mononuclear phagocyte / reticuloendothelial system; antigen presenters to T cells
Leukocytes, plasma cellsarrive from blood; plasma cell = B-cell derivative
Adipocytefat dissolved in processing → empty space, thin rim, flattened peripheral nucleus. White fat: hypodermis, triglycerides/cholesterol esters, cushioning. Brown fat: around organs, thermal insulation. Frozen section + Oil Red O shows lipid bright red
Clinical note — Pataday

Olopatadine 0.2% (now OTC) stabilises the mast cell membrane so granules cannot fuse and release histamine — preventive, not a post-hoc antihistamine. Stop degranulation and the allergic cascade never starts.

4. Ground substance

A strict hierarchy: hyaluronan backbone → link protein → core proteins → GAGs as bottle-brush bristles. Core protein + its GAGs = one proteoglycan monomer, interwoven among collagen fibrils. GAGs (formerly mucopolysaccharides): chondroitin, dermatan, heparan, keratan sulfate. Large proteoglycans: versican, perlecan, neurocan, aggrecan (cartilage and sclera), brevican. Small: decorin, biglycan, testican, bikunin, fibromodulin, lumican.

Hyaluronan is the odd one: non-sulfated, made in the plasma membrane not the Golgi, molecular weight in the millions, holds water like a sponge (the gel character); ~15 g per 70 kg person, a third turned over every day; roles in proliferation, migration, some tumours.

Eye link — the vitreous

The vitreous is essentially a hyaluronan gel holding water, with only a sparse collagen framework — hence transparent and gel-like.
